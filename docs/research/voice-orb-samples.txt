Voice orb — captured per-frame samples
======================================

The unreduced measurement behind K1, K2 and K3, for all six transitions and
three mid-transition interrupts. Every value is a getComputedStyle or
getBoundingClientRect reading taken on that frame, in headless Chrome, against
docs/research/voice-orb-mock.html. Nothing is read out of the engine, and no
frames are omitted — each block is contiguous from three settled frames before
the state change through to arrival.

Regenerate:  node docs/research/voice-orb-evidence.mjs --raw

Columns
  ms          milliseconds since setState; "--" marks the settled frames before it
  rect        getBoundingClientRect().width / computed width — the rendered box,
              as a multiple of its untransformed size. This is the only honest
              geometric reading: getComputedStyle().transform omits the
              independent `scale` property.
  d(rect)     change since the previous frame
  glow        computed opacity of .orb-halos. No oscillator acts on it, so its
              series is the pure state blend and the K2 interval test on it is exact.
  d(glow)     change since the previous frame
  tint[from]  computed opacity of the outgoing state's tint layer
  tint[to]    computed opacity of the incoming state's tint layer
  colour      the composited orb colour, from the measured layer opacities
  K2          whether this frame lies inside the interval between the two states
              ("-" on interrupt runs, where three states are involved)
