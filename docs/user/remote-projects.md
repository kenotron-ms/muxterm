# Open Muxterm on another screen

Your chats, files, and Work stay on the computer running Muxterm. To check in from a phone or another computer, keep Muxterm running and open its reachable address in that device’s browser. The local address that works on the host computer may be reachable only from that computer.

## Check in from your phone

1. Open your Muxterm address and sign in.
2. Use the menu to return to a recent conversation. Open **Work** to see what needs a review or response.
3. To keep Muxterm handy, use your browser’s **Add to Home Screen** or **Install app** action if offered.

If the address doesn’t load, check that the host computer is awake and that your device can reach it. If someone else set up Muxterm, ask them for the address to use.

## Open a project on another computer

If you already use SSH to access a project machine, Muxterm can keep your files and agent sign-in on that machine while you open its chats from your usual Muxterm address. To connect the machine, follow [remote projects and chats](../guides/remote-projects-and-chats.md). Once it’s connected, use the location picker in **New chat** to choose its project folder.
