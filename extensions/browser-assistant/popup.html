Browser access

Paused

Turn on to open a dedicated browser window for your agents.

Settings & permissions

Access stays on until you pause it. Browser restarts leave it paused. Pairing stays connected.

Agents on this Muxterm account can read, screenshot, navigate, click and type in the dedicated window, including signed-in sites. They can submit forms and make changes. Other windows are outside their control.

“Control this tab” limits access to its current site. Chrome shows its debugging banner while control is active.

Local control. Pause whenever you want.
